Research Plan
===============

The body of a research plan is a list of entries: texts, tables, images, structures (ketcher),
samples and reactions. New texts, tables and images can be added with
:meth:`chemotion_api.elements.research_plan.ResearchPlan.add_richtext`,
:meth:`chemotion_api.elements.research_plan.ResearchPlan.add_table` and
:meth:`chemotion_api.elements.research_plan.ResearchPlan.add_image`.
